The frontmatter title supplies the page title. Start body sections at level two so they sit beneath it in the document hierarchy.

## Prepare a workspace

The table of contents includes this section and its children. Select an entry to jump to its heading.

### Choose a folder

Store wiki pages under `public/wiki`. Folder paths become groups in the navigation.

#### Name a page

Use a descriptive filename such as `release-checklist.md`. The frontmatter title can use natural language and spaces.

### Add metadata

Every page needs a non-empty `title`. An `author` is optional.

## Review the page

### Check section links

Use [this same-page link](#prepare-a-workspace) to return to the first section. Notice the heading anchor in the browser address bar.

### Inspect the hierarchy

Compare the level-two, level-three, and level-four headings in the body with their indentation in the table of contents.

## Try it

Navigate through the section links with the keyboard. A page with no body headings, such as the **Minimal page** example, has no empty table of contents.
